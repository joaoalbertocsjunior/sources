sources-lib follows the [Linux Foundation Minimum Viable Governance (MVG)](https://github.com/github/MVG) model.

## Decision making

Decisions are made by maintainers via GitHub issues and pull requests. For significant changes (new adapters, breaking API changes, roadmap shifts), a proposal issue must be open for at least 7 days before merging.

Maintainers may be added by unanimous agreement of existing maintainers. A maintainer who is inactive for 6 months may be moved to emeritus status.

## Contributions

All contributions are made via pull request. At least one maintainer approval is required to merge. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the contribution process.

## Changes to governance

Changes to this document require a pull request open for at least 14 days and approval from all active maintainers.
